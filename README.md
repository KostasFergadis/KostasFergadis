# Hi, I'm Kostas

Full-stack developer in Athens. For the past 2 years I've worked at Sklavenitis, one of Greece's largest supermarket chains, on its online store, internal portal and delivery platform integrations.

## What I work on

- **Delivery platform sync:** keeping tens of thousands of products in sync with eFood, Wolt and Box. I made the catalogue import more reliable with limited concurrency, retries with backoff, and a fallback to the last good copy.
- **Debugging in production:** tracing failed requests through logs, finding affected products with SQL, and fixing causes like a MySQL upsert that silently dropped updates.
- **Internal portal:** moving a standalone security operations app into the company portal, page by page, with PHP classes, CRUD screens and DataTables views.
- **E-commerce:** 100+ tickets on the Laravel store, from Figma designs to cart, delivery address and checkout features.

## Stack

**Also used:** Python, Django, React, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, MongoDB
